BLOCKFUSE Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10 · Publisher: Michael Muller · Contact: mg.muller@outlook.com

Summary

BLOCKFUSE is a block puzzle game. It has no accounts and needs no sign-in. Your game progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which processes some device data to show and measure ads, as described below. Buying Premium removes ads.

Data the game keeps on your device

  • Game progress and settings: scores, levels, puzzle album, themes, mochi buddy looks, achievements and your settings.
  • A local usage log of game events (for example "level finished"), kept in the app's private storage. It is not sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & data → Gameplay analytics.
  • A random install number used only to vary game boards. It is not linked to you.
  • Whether you own Premium, so ads stay off.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a short ad between games — never during play, and never during the first day after you install the game. Rewarded videos are optional. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), ad interactions (such as views and taps) and diagnostic information. Google uses this data under its own privacy policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

  • Your consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & data → Ad privacy choices.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Ads never see your board, your moves or your game data, and the game's piece generator never looks at ads, purchases or spending.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The game only records on your device that Premium is owned; purchases can be restored from the store.

What else leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to: Share (a result card you send yourself), Export my data (copied to your clipboard), or Rate the game (opens the store page). Reminders are scheduled on your device; no push server is used.

Your choices and rights

  • Export my data: Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data.
  • Delete my data: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my data erases everything the game stores on this device. Uninstalling the game also removes it.
  • Ad data: use Ad privacy choices in the game and your device's ad settings; for data held by Google, see Google's policy above.
  • You can contact us at mg.muller@outlook.com with any privacy question or request.

Children

BLOCKFUSE is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit. Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and the game will ask for consent again where the law requires it.
